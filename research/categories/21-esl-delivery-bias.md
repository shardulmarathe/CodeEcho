# Category 21 — ESL / delivery-metric bias

**Evidence:** [`../notes/21-esl-delivery-bias.md`](../notes/21-esl-delivery-bias.md)

## Bottom line

Filler/WPM/pause metrics can **unfairly punish L2 speakers**. Accent ≠ intelligibility; L2 pause patterns differ from native norms; ASR error is higher on accented speech and can inflate filler/WPM. Keep **Content ≠ Delivery**; disclose Delivery is not an accent score; never fold pace into one overall quality number.

## Recommended CodeEcho actions

1. UI: lead recruiter/report view with content dimensions; Delivery clearly secondary.
2. Disclose ASR may err on accents; allow transcript correction before scoring when feasible.
3. Avoid native WPM pass/fail thresholds; prefer mid-clause pause signals if used.
4. Never ship “vs native speaker” acoustic scoring on free tier.
